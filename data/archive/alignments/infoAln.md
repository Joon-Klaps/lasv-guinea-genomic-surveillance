- the “1°” alignments: basically all available sequences if quality (Raph did re-check the quality of all of them)

- the “2°” alignments: the 1st ones minus some sequences for which there were the high suspicion of a duplicate (we only keep one sequences per pair of duplicates), the suspicion being determined according to a script looking at nucleotide mismatches

- the “3°” alignments: the 2nd ones minus all sequences for which the sampling origin was not enough precise to continuous phylogeographic inference
